# Look metrics: v2 crystal cut (captures/trailer-crystal-v2.mp4)

Generated by `demo3d look-metrics` (demo3d/tools/src/demo3d_tools/look.py) against the palette
`v2-palette.json` and the thresholds below. L* is CIE L*, C*ab and hue are CIELAB (D65).

- **Bright, conformance, families**: bright is the share of pixels at L* >= 50; conformance is
  the share of them within 20 degrees of a palette role's hue (neutral ones, C*ab < 15, when
  the palette has a white); families is the hue peaks (30-degree bins) holding 3% of
  the bright chromatic pixels.
- **Value structure**: emitted is the share at L* >= 60 or C*ab >= 45; the floor is every other
  pixel (its L* p50 and p90); clipped is every channel >= 250.
- **Swarm separation**: in each 64 px tile (at 1920 wide) that one enemy hue covers at least
  50% of (C*ab >= 30, L* >= 25, gaps up to 4 px closed), the share of
  that clump's pixels on a boundary (L* spanning 30 in a 3x3 window); separation is the
  dense tiles' median, p10 their 10th percentile. The `-swarm.jpg` overlays outline every dense tile.

Rules (provisional: ps-wnj.2's starting values from neon-look.md's examples and the v2 calibration (demo3d/evidence/look/metrics); the style guide (ps-wnj.1, neon-look-style.md) sets the real ones by editing this file):

- `conformance` >= 0.9: the bright pixels sit on the palette's roles
- `families` <= 4: a hue budget: at most four hue families plus white in a frame
- `floorP50` <= 8: the floor is near black
- `floorP90` <= 20: outside ripples and light pools the floor stays dark
- `emitted` <= 0.3: light comes from emitters; the dark stays dark
- `clipped` <= 0.02: white is earned: at most 2% clipped outside impact frames
- `separation` >= 0.3: a dense clump of one type reads as individuals, not a blob
