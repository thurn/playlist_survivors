# Look metrics: hero-edge

Generated by `demo3d look-metrics` (demo3d/tools/src/demo3d_tools/look.py) against the palette
`ion.json`, preset `edge`, and the rules of
docs/archive/design/neon-look-style.md (Rules and Thresholds), on stills scaled to 960 px wide. L* and
C*ab are CIELAB (D65), hues HSV. Blank cells: the still lacks what the measure needs (an ID render, a
view json, an emitter, a dense clump).

Rules (docs/archive/design/neon-look-style.md, Rules and Thresholds (ps-wnj.1); halo_min, monster_form_min and swarm_separation_min calibrated on v2 by ps-wnj.21 (demo3d/evidence/look/metrics); the restraint rules (floor_p99, background_light, flash_frames) by ps-wnj.24 (game/evidence/look/restraint, demo3d/evidence/look/restraint)):

- `floor_p50_max` (floor_p50 <= 8): the floor is void
- `floor_p90_max` (floor_p90 <= 30): rings and breaths included, the floor stays under ground light
- `floor_p99_max` (floor_p99 <= 20): the grid's lines are a quiet background (ps-wnj.24); prism's gems halo onto the floor
- `background_light_max` (background_light <= 0.15): the world never drowns the swarm: the background carries a small share of the frame's light (ps-wnj.24)
- `frame_p25_max` (frame_p25 <= 6, unless the still has floor_p50): the video fallback for the floor: the darkest quarter is void
- `black_share_min` (black_share >= 0.3): a swarm is lit things with dark between them
- `bright_share_max` (bright_share <= 0.22): light comes from emitters, not a lit world
- `bright_share_min` (bright_share >= 0.04): something emits
- `clipped_max` (clipped <= 0.02): white is earned
- `clipped_impact_max` (clipped <= 0.12 (impact frames)): even an impact frame is not a white-out
- `clipped_blob_max` (clipped_blob <= 0.005): no blown-out region outside impact frames
- `white_max` (white <= 0.02): white is earned: impact frames, sparks and the blade's flare
- `halo_min` (halo >= 15): emitters glow: their surroundings sit well over the dark
- `families_max` (families <= 4): the hue budget: the zone's key and three enemy families
- `key60_min` (key60 >= 0.55): each frame has a colour key
- `key60_median_min` (key60_median >= 0.62 (the cut)): a cut keys as GW3 does
- `palette_conformance_min` (palette_conformance >= 0.85): everything drawn takes its colour from a role
- `shot_floor_range_max` (shot_floor_range <= 6 (the cut)): one world across the shots, not stitched parts
- `flash_frames_max` (flash_frames <= 0.5 (the cut)): flashes are for special moments: at most half the steady stills hold a near-white region over 0.3% of the frame (ps-wnj.24)
- `hero_margin_min` (hero_margin >= 5): the hero is the brightest thing on screen
- `monster_form_min` (monster_form >= 30): a monster shows form, not flat paint
- `swarm_separation_min` (swarm_separation >= 13): a dense clump of one type reads as individuals, not a blob
- `readability_min` (readability >= 17): monsters stand off the ground round them (TD15)
