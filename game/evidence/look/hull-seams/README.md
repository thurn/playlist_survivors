`game look ps31-PRESET`) re-captured with ps-wnj.31's change and measured by
its look.py. The change, and the crystal cut's numbers, are in
[demo3d/evidence/look/hull-seams](../../../../demo3d/evidence/look/hull-seams/README.md).

`monster_form_min`: edge 15.7-33.2, solid 17.4-29.7, hologram 15.9-23.6,
prism 13.7-27.5. The only break is hologram's background light at 58 s
(0.162), which ps-wnj.30 already recorded.
